## 1. Purpose & Governance Rules

This register governs all technical, scientific, operational, and regulatory statements made across the Umbrella codebase, user interfaces, documentation, and live jury presentations.

### Core Governance Rules:
1. **Never conflate physical risk with financial exposure.** Physical climate hazard is driven by rainfall, topography, and hydrology—not loan outstanding.
2. **Never claim unverified carbon offsets.** All emissions calculations are uncertified operational proxies, not voluntary market or compliance offsets.
3. **Never claim blockchain, crypto, or autonomous AI disbursements.** Audit trails are append-only application logs with SHA-256 evidence digests; every financial action requires human approval.
4. **Never overstate production maturity.** Umbrella is an audited, tested, demo-hardened hackathon MVP pilot.
